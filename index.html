About

I lead a software development team working on autonomous driving and robotics at TIER IV, built around Autoware and ROS 2. My work covers the full path from requirements and architecture to implementation, field testing, and integration into commercial products running on real vehicles. My current focus is off-road autonomous driving — extending Autoware-based systems to construction sites, including large autonomous dump trucks and LiDAR-based perception.
